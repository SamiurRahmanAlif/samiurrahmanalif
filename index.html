About

Background and research pillars

I completed a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at North South University in December 2025, and now work as a Research Assistant at SEAL (Systems on Embodied AI Lab) in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering. My work spans two threads: the reliability of multimodal models under linguistic change, and the large‑scale data engineering needed to evaluate them properly.

Outside the lab, I served as General Secretary and now Student Advisor of the NSU Model United Nations Club, and in June 2026 completed an unsupported seven‑day trek to Everest Base Camp.
